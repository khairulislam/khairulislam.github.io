This course covers the wireless sensing basics (e.g., radar, Wi-Fi) and cutting-edge applications (e.g., motion tracking, activity recognition, environmental sensing). Core technologies covered include radar signal processing, Wi-Fi sensing (OFDM, MIMO, Channel State Information), and mmWave/CmWave sensing. Course instructor: Professor Kun Qian. The course website is [here](https://kunqian.info/teaching/uva_cs6501_ws4iot/fall2024/).
